# Document Generation

This context prepares insurance application forms from applicant-submitted information and keeps a record of each successfully generated form request.

## Participants and records

**Applicant**:
The person seeking insurance coverage and providing the information in the application.

**Premium Payer**:
The person responsible for paying the insurance premium; this may be the applicant or another person.

**Beneficiary**:
A person designated to receive the policy benefit.

**Agent**:
A licensed representative who assists with an insurance application.

**Application ID**:
The identifier assigned to one insurance application.

**Application Form**:
The formal record of an applicant's requested coverage, answers, declarations, and consents.

**Request Archive**:
The preserved information submitted for one successful form generation.
